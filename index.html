Beautiful Spots in the Philippines

Discover five breathtaking destinations worth visiting, from island lagoons to mountain terraces.

Introduction

The Philippines has more than 7,000 islands, and each one has its own story. Here are five spots every traveler should see: turquoise lagoons, ancient terraces, shipwreck waters, a Spanish-era town and a sea of tiny islands.

Palawan

El Nido

El Nido sits at the northern tip of Palawan and is famous for dramatic limestone karsts, hidden lagoons and powder-white beaches. Island hopping tours lead to the Big Lagoon, Small Lagoon and Secret Lagoon, which you can reach by kayak or by swimming through a rock opening.

Best time: Nov to MayDo: island hopping, kayaking

Ifugao

Banaue Rice Terraces

Cut into the Cordillera mountains by the Ifugao people, these terraces step down the slopes like a giant green staircase. They are often called the Eighth Wonder of the World and are part of a UNESCO World Heritage listing. The views are best in planting and harvest seasons.

Best time: Mar to MayDo: trekking, village visits

Palawan

Coron

Coron mixes lakes, hot springs and some of the best wreck diving in Asia, thanks to Japanese ships sunk in World War II. Crystal-clear Kayangan Lake and the twin lagoons are highlights of any Coron island tour.

Best time: Dec to MayDo: wreck diving, hot springs

Ilocos Sur

Vigan

Vigan is one of the best-preserved Spanish colonial towns in Asia. Walk its cobblestone streets, ride a horse-drawn kalesa past ancestral houses, and taste local empanada and longganisa. It is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Best time: Nov to FebDo: kalesa ride, food trip

Pangasinan

Hundred Islands

This national park in Alaminos has 124 islands and islets at low tide. Visitors can kayak, snorkel, zip-line across to Governor Island and relax on quiet beaches. It is an easy weekend trip from Manila.

Best time: Nov to MayDo: snorkeling, zip line
